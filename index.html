📖 Knowledge & Security FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about client-side PDF encryption, Indian document passwords, and air-gapped security.

How do I unlock an e-Aadhaar PDF without uploading it to random websites? ▼

The standard UIDAI e-Aadhaar password consists of 8 characters: the first 4 letters of your NAME in UPPERCASE followed by your 4-digit Year of Birth (e.g. SATY1998 or ANIT1975). Simply drop your locked PDF into VaultPDF, enter the password, and click Decrypt. Your PDF is decrypted in your browser's RAM and saved as a permanently clean, unprotected PDF.

Is VaultPDF safe for confidential bank statements and tax records? ▼

Yes, 100% safe. VaultPDF is built with a Zero-Knowledge Architecture. Unlike traditional tools that upload your sensitive documents to remote cloud servers, VaultPDF runs entirely inside your browser sandbox via WebAssembly and the native Web Crypto API. Zero bytes are transmitted across the internet.

Why is client-side decryption better than iLovePDF, Smallpdf, or Adobe Online? ▼

Cloud converters require uploading your entire PDF over the internet to their servers. This exposes your PAN, bank account numbers, balances, employer details, and personal address to third-party databases. VaultPDF eliminates this vulnerability completely by processing files locally on your machine with zero server roundtrips.

Can I combine multiple PDF documents together with image files (JPG, PNG, WebP)? ▼

Yes! In the Merge PDFs & Images tab, you can drop any mix of PDF files, camera photos, scanned certificates, and receipts (JPG, PNG, WebP, etc.). VaultPDF automatically fits each image to a standard high-resolution page with preserved aspect ratio, lets you reorder files freely, and merges everything into one professional PDF document with 0 server uploads.

Can VaultPDF work completely offline without internet? ▼

Yes! Once the page is loaded, you can turn off Wi-Fi or mobile data completely. All cryptographic decryption, AES-256 encryption, page merging, and splitting will execute at full speed offline.

What standard password formats do Indian banks use? ▼

• SBI: Last 5 digits of registered mobile number + Date of Birth (DDMM)
• HDFC: NetBanking Customer ID or PAN (UPPERCASE)
• ICICI: First 4 letters of name (lowercase) + DDMM of birth
• PNB: Full 16-digit account number without spaces
• Form 16 / Salary Slips: Full PAN number in UPPERCASE
